Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Cirrus is a weather app built on a simple principle: give you the weather without tracking you. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

What we collect

We collect only what's necessary to run the service:

  • Email address — When you subscribe or redeem a beta code. Used to manage your subscription and send weather briefings you've opted into.
  • Location data — If you tap "Use My Location," your device's GPS coordinates are used to fetch your local weather. We do not store or log your location. It is sent directly from your device to the weather API and never passes through our servers.
  • Subscription status — Whether your subscription is active, and what plan you're on. Stored securely in our database to grant access to the app.
  • Push notification preferences — If you opt into morning briefings, evening updates, or severe alerts, we store your city and notification preferences via OneSignal to deliver those messages.

What we do not collect

  • We do not run ads and have no advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell your data to any third party.
  • We do not collect browsing history, device identifiers beyond what OneSignal requires, or behavioral analytics.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking. Local preferences (dark mode, units, recent searches) are stored only in your browser's local storage and never sent to us.

Third-party services

Cirrus uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Stripe — Processes subscription payments. Stripe handles all payment card data; we never see or store your card details. Stripe's privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.
  • OneSignal — Delivers push notifications. Only used if you opt into notifications. OneSignal's privacy policy is at onesignal.com/privacy_policy.
  • Xweather (AerisWeather) — Provides weather forecast, radar, and observation data. Your coordinates are sent to their API to retrieve weather conditions. See xweather.com/privacy.
  • Cloudflare — Hosts our backend API. Cloudflare may log request metadata (IP addresses, timestamps) for security and abuse prevention. See cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

How we use your email

Your email is used for:

  • Managing your subscription (confirmations, billing notices, cancellation)
  • Sending weather briefings you've explicitly requested in Settings
  • Responding to support requests you initiate

We do not send marketing emails or newsletters. You can cancel your subscription at any time through Settings → Manage Subscription.

Data retention

We retain your email and subscription status for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for billing and legal purposes. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

Children's privacy

Cirrus is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

AI Features

Weather summaries and "What to Wear" recommendations are generated by Anthropic's Claude AI. To produce these, your approximate location and current weather conditions are sent to Anthropic's API. No personally identifiable information is included in these requests. Anthropic's privacy policy is at anthropic.com/privacy.
